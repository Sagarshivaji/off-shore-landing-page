Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Offshore Technology Teams?

Learn more about how flexible offshore talent can complement your engineering and delivery requirements.

What is offshore delivery support?
Offshore delivery support involves extending your engineering and QA capabilities by leveraging dedicated teams located in different geographic regions. This model allows you to scale your technical workforce, access specialized talent, and accelerate delivery without the overhead of building a local infrastructure from scratch.
What technology capabilities can be supported?
We support a wide range of capabilities including software engineering, QA automation, performance testing, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), AI/ML (GenAI, RAG, machine learning engineering), and modern data ecosystems. Our talent is sourced to match your specific technical stack.
Can offshore talent work with our existing teams?
Absolutely. Our engagement models are designed for seamless integration. Offshore team members participate in your daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and code reviews, aligning directly with your existing workflows and product stakeholders.
Can the team scale as our requirements change?
Yes. One of the primary benefits of our offshore delivery model is flexibility. Whether you need to expand your team for a new project phase or scale down after a major release, we can adjust the team structure to match your evolving needs.
How can we learn more about Techgene's experience?
You can review our detailed case studies linked in the section above, or book a direct consultation with our team. We are happy to walk you through our past engagements, delivery models, and specific technical successes.
